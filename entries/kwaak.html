Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 207 #2: ？緙꜆ Kwák꜆ (gwaak1); here corrected to Kwʻák꜆ (kwaak1) and normalised to 𡃈)

    A colloquial word;

    • a loop,
    • a bight of a rope;
    • to tie up with a loop;
    • a stroll;
    • ꜂tá yat꜆ ko꜄ kwʻák꜆ (daa2 jat1 go3 kwaak1 打一個𡃈) make a noose;
    • kwʻák꜆ chü꜅ ꜃kʻü (kwaak1 zyu6 keoi5 𡃈住佢), catch him in a lasso, noose him;
    • ꜁tʻung ꜃ní hü꜄ kwʻák꜆ ꜃há (tung4 nei5 heoi3 kwaak1 haa5 同你去𡃈吓), let us take a turn.
Conway's commentary
  • All of Williams' examples correctly read kwʻák꜆ (kwaak1 or kwaak3) despite the entry being erroneously placed under kwák꜆ (gwaak3).
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【𡃈】 (kwaak1)
    1. An enclosing shape or loop.
      • 四方𡃈 (sei3 fong1 kwaak1): "Square enclosing-shape."
    2. To enclose or loop.

The spelling 𡃈 is arbitrary.
