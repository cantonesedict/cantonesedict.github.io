Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 173 #9: ꜂噉)
    • An adverb of quality, so, thus, this manner;
    • an interjection of surprise;
    • ꜂kòm ꜃yéung yéung꜅ tsò꜅ tak꜆ (gam2 joeng6-2 zou6 dak1 噉樣做得) this will do, very well;
    • tsau꜅ ꜂kòm (zau6 gam2 就噉), stop, well;
    • ꜂kòm ꜃yéung yéung꜅ ꜂tsz' (gam2 joeng6 zi2 噉樣子) such a style! used in depreciation;
    • ꜂kòm wá꜅ (gam2 waa6 噉話) if you say so, if it be so;
    • ꜀tʻung ꜀tò hai꜅ ꜂kòm ké꜄ (tung1 dou1 hai6 gam2 ge3 通都係噉嘅) they are all just so, all alike;
    • ꜂kòm ké꜄! ꜁'m ꜂hò ꜂kòm (gam2 ge3-2! m4 hou2 gam2 噉嘅、唔好噉), so! don't do so, that'll not do;
    • ꜂kòm ꜂tám tsò꜅ (gam2 daam2 zou6 噉膽做) so daring, brave.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 噉樣做得: Corrected ꜃yéung (joeng5) to yéung꜅ (joeng6-2).
  • Williams' 噉樣子: Corrected ꜃yéung (joeng5) to yéung꜅ (joeng6). A more modern equivalent would be 噉嘅樣 gam2 ge3 joeng6-2.
  • Williams' 噉膽做: Better rendered "[to] do [with] gall like so". Not the best example with which to demonstrate 噉.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【噉】 (gam2)
    1. Like so, like that, in such a manner, this way.
      • 噉樣 (gam2 joeng6-2): "Like so."
      • 噉嘅樣 (gam2 ge3 joeng6-2): "Looking like that."
      • 爛成噉 (laan6 seng4 gam2): "Broken to such [a degree]."
      • 就噉 (zau6 gam2): "Leave it at that."
      • 噉點得㗎 (gam2 dim2 dak1 gaa3): "How [is] this acceptable?"
      • 噉至得咖嘛 (gam2 zi3 dak1 ga1 maa3): "Only like so [will it be] acceptable."
      • 成個乞兒噉 (seng4 go3 hat1 ji4-1 gam2): "Like [a] complete beggar."
      • 如果你好似狗一樣噉食嘢 (jyu4 gwo2 nei5 hou2 ci5 gau2 jat1 joeng6 gam2 sik6 je5): "If you [will] eat things in such [a] manner [that is the] same as [a] dog" (see Kill Bill: Volume 2).
    2. Used conjunctively.
      • 噉走㗎喇 (gam2 zau2 ga3 laa3): "So then we'll [be] leaving."
  • 【係噉先】 (hai6 gam2 sin1)
    1. Used to wrap up a conversation. (Literally, "[let it] be like so [at] first".)

The word 噉 gam2 ("like so") is conservatively pronounced [kom], not [kɐm]. (Arguably, it would be better to romanise the conservative pronunciation as gum2 rather than gam2, but standard Jyutping does not permit this.)

Alongside 咁 gam3 ("so/that"), 噉 gam2 ("like so") is one of the few words with a Williams final of òm that has resisted the shift to am in modern Cantonese.

The spellings 噉 and 咁 for gam2 are arbitrary (but well established).
