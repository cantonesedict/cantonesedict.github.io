Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜀亨)
    • To penetrate or pervade (Kangxi: 通也);
    • successful;
    • ꜀hang ꜀kʻü (hang1 keoi1 亨區), a quiet street, an eligible street.

  • 【亨】 (xaeng)
    • (adjective-like) penetrating

哼 ꜀Hang (hang1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼝ + 7
Unicode code point
U+54FC
Fan Wan entry
(Not present)
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜀哼)
    • Frightened, an antipathy to (Kangxi: 嗆哼愚怯貌);
    • a local word applied to smells, lothsome.
Conway's commentary
  • Commonly used to spell the interjection hm1 or hng1 (equivalent to English hmph!).
  • Williams' "a local word applied to smells": Eitel (1877) gives the example 臭哼哼 chʻau꜄ ꜀hang ꜀hang (cau3 hang1 hang1), explained "a nasty smell".
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To hum.
