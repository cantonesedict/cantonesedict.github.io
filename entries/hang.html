Fan Wan entry
(Not present)
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜀哼)
    • Frightened, an antipathy to (Kangxi: 嗆哼愚怯貌);

Conway's commentary
  • Commonly used to spell the interjection hm1 or hng1 (equivalent to English hmph!).

行 ꜁Hang (hang4)

Radical + residual strokes
⾏ + 0
Unicode code point
U+884C
Alternative forms
  • 恆 (for Cantonese "taut/maximally" sense)
  • 恒 (for Cantonese "taut/maximally" sense)
  • 衡 (for Cantonese "taut/maximally" sense)
Reading variations
  • haang4 (vernacular for "walk" and "move" senses)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 30 行) 行步踐也履也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 73 before ꜁行)

    (This character (行) is often pronounced like the next syllable (haang).)

  • (Page 73 ꜁行)
    • To go, to walk, to proceed (Kangxi: 人之步趨也, 適也往也去也);
    • to act, to do, to direct;
    • imports the action of the following verb;
    • to appeal, or transfer a case;
    • a step;
    • a road (Kangxi: 路也), a way (Kangxi: 道也), a path;
    • one of the elements (Kangxi: 五行 etc.);
    • a manner;
    • motion;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng lò꜅ (haang4 lou6 行路) to walk in the way;
    • pò꜅ ꜁hang (bou6 hang4 步行), to travel afoot;
    • ꜁hang ꜁yan (hang4 jan4 行人), a traveler;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜃lai (haang4 lai5 行禮), to visit, to attend to prescribed rites;
    • ꜁hang ꜃ü (hang4 jyu5 行語), to tell to;
    • ꜁hang ꜃lí (hang4 lei5 行李), baggage;
    • ꜃'ng ꜁hang (ng5 hang4 五行), the 5 elements;
    • ꜁hang káu꜄ (hang4 gaau3 行教) to promulgate;
    • tái꜅ ꜁hang (daai6 hang4 大行), the recently deceased emperor or empress;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜁tʻau (haang4 tau4 行頭), head-gear, masks, &c., used by actors;
    • ꜁hang ꜀héung (hang4 hoeng1 行香), to go in procession;
    • ꜁hang ꜀tsʻing (hang4 cing1 行清), to worship at the tombs in spring;
    • ꜁hang ꜁ying (hang4 jing4 行刑), to punish;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜀tsʻín pò꜅ (haang4 cin1 bou6 行千步) to 'walk a mile;'
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜁shün (haang4 syun4-2 行船), to weigh anchor, to follow the sea, to act as a sailor;
    • ꜀hang ꜁háng ꜁mái tik꜆ (haang4 maai4 dik1 行埋滴) step aside a little!
    • ꜁hang ꜂hím (hang4 him2 行險), to follow dangerous pursuits;
    • ꜁hang ꜀ping (hang4 bing1 行兵), to direct troops;
    • ꜁hang ꜁wai (hang4 wai4 行為), conduct, manner of acting;
    • ꜁hang ling꜅ (hang4 ling6 行令) to send an order;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜃há ꜂ché ché꜄ (haang4 haa5 ze3 行吓嗻), wait, or do; a little presently;
    • ꜁hang ꜀shü (hang4 syu1 行書), to write a running hand,;
    • ꜁hang ꜁háng ꜁lúi (haang4 leoi4 行雷), to thunder.
  • (Page 716 ꜁行)
    • Temporary, for the present;
    • ꜁hang ꜁ying (hang4 jing4 行營), a movable or flying camp.

    Read hang꜅ (hang6) in colloquial.

    • Tense, taut, drawn tight, as a drum or umbrella.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' ꜁hang (hang4) has been normalised to vernacular ꜁háng (haang4) where appropriate.
  • Williams' 行路: Or more generally, to walk. To be distinguished run 走, to run.
  • Williams' 行刑: Better rendered "to carry out punishment".
  • Williams' 行兵: Also ꜁háng ꜀ping (haang4 bing1), to move a pawn (in Chinese Chess).
  • Williams' 行吓嗻: Corrected ꜂ché (ze2) to ché꜄ (ze3). In modern Cantonese the final particle would be 啫 ze1 instead of the (now) dated 嗻 ze3.
  • Williams' "Read […] in colloquial": The reading hang꜅ (hang6) for Cantonese "taut" appears to be obsolete.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. The 144th radical (U+2F8F ⾏ "walk-enclosure").
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【行】 (haeng)
    • (verb-like) [to] walk, [to] move, (met.) journey
    • (verb-like) [to] perform, [to] enact
    • (noun-like) walk-[way]
    • (noun-like) (met.) element
Conway's Cantonese entry

Cantonese retains the classical distinction between 行 "walk" and 走 "run".

  • 【行】 (haang4)
    1. To walk.
      • 行得慢 (haang4 dak1 maan6): "[To] walk slowly."
      • 行得正企得正 (haang4 dak1 zeng3 kei5 dak1 zeng3): "[He] walks properly [and] stands properly" (i.e. he is beyond reproach).
    2. To move, to go.
      • 行得慢 (haang4 dak1 maan6): "[To] move slowly" (of a vehicle).
      • 好行喇 (hou2 haang4 laa3): "[We'd] better make [a] move".
  • 【行街】 (haang4 gaai1)
    1. To walk around in the street (e.g. on a date).
    2. (adjective) Takeaway.
  • 【行行企企】 (haang4 haang4 kei5 kei5)
    1. To stand around with little to do. (Literally, "walk walk stand stand".)
  • 【行路】 (haang4 lou6)
    1. To walk.
      • 行路去 (haang4 lou6 heoi3): "Go [by] walking" (as opposed to driving).
  • 【行】 (hang4)
    1. Taut.
      • 唔夠行 (m4 gau3 hang4): "Not taut enough."
      • 扯行啲 (ce2 hang4 di1): "Pull [it] tauter."
    2. Maximally.
      • 踹行油 (jaai2 hang4 jau4-2): "Stepping fully on [the] accelerator" (i.e. flooring it).
      • 開行冷氣 (hoi1 hang4 laang5 hei3): "Running air-con at maximum."

The spelling 行 for hang4 meaning "taut" or "maximally" is arbitrary.
