克 Hak꜆ (hak1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼉ + 5
Unicode code point
U+514B
Alternative forms
  • 剋
  • 勀
  • 勊
  • 尅
Reading variations
  • haak1 (longer syllable)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 33 克) [能]也勝也又聚斂曰[掊]克
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 克꜆)
    • To sustain, to carry (Kangxi: 肩也), adequate to (Kangxi: 肩任也任者又負荷之名也能勝此物謂之克也, 克能也);
    • superior to;
    • to subdue, to conquer (Kangxi: 勝也);
    • (…);
    • hak꜆ chai꜄ (hak1 zai3 克制) to order, to make one obey;
    • hak꜆ ꜂fú (hak1 fu2 克苦), hurried, oppressed;
    • fat꜆ hak꜆ ꜀shing yam꜅ (fat1 hak1 sing1 jam6 弗克勝任) inadequate for the place;
    • hak꜆ ꜀tong (hak1 dong1 克當), to sustain, fit for;
    • hak꜆ ꜂kí (hak1 gei2 克己), to do what you ask others, self-government.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 剋. See 剋 hak1 for the canonical entry.)

  • (Page 70 尅꜆ Hák꜆ (haak1); here normalised to 克 Hak꜆ (hak1))
    • To subdue, to overcome (Kangxi 剋: 剋勝也, 謂得勝也);
    • (…).
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing stroke in 能 and corrected 拮 to 掊.
  • Williams' "to subdue, to conquer": This requires Kangxi 勝也 to be departing-tone.
  • Williams' 克制: In modern usage, a reflexive "restrain" rather than a transitive "subdue".
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To subdue (oneself), to restrain (Kangxi: 勝己之私謂之克).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【克】 (khok)
    • (verb-like) [to] sustain, [to] carry
    • (adjective-like) capable
    • (verb-like) [to] overcome, [to] vanquish
    • (verb-like) [to] subdue

刻 Hak꜆ (hak1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼑ + 6
Unicode code point
U+523B
Alternative forms
  • 剋 (for all senses except time-unit sense)
Reading variations
  • haak1 (longer syllable)
  • kaak1 (lazy elision in 即刻)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 33 刻) [時]刻[刋]刻[鏤]也[刺]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 刻꜆ Hák꜆ (haak1); here normalised to Hak꜆ (hak1))
    • To carve (Kangxi: 鏤也), to chisel, to sculpture;
    • a to cut (Kangxi: 割也);
    • griping (Kangxi: 一曰痛也), close;
    • fifteen minutes, a little while (Kangxi: 刻漏也 etc.);
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ tsz'꜅ (hak1 zi6 刻字) to cut characters;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ ꜂pán (hak1 baan2 刻版), to cut blocks;
    • hán꜅ hák꜆ hak꜆ (haan6 hak1 限刻) a limit, a set time;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ kwat꜆ ꜃ming ꜀sam (hak1 gwat1 ming5 sam1 刻骨銘心), graven on the bones and inscribed in the heart;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ há꜅ (hak1 haa6) presently;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ yat꜇ (hak1 jat6 刻日) a set day;
    • ꜁shí hák꜆ hak꜆ (si4 hak1 時刻) constant, usual;
    • ꜂shiú hák꜆ hak꜆ (siu2 hak1 少刻) a little time.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected ⿱艹⿰丩寸 to 時, corrected 利 to 刋, corrected 鏌 to 鏤, and corrected 剌 to 刺.
  • Williams' 刻日: That is, an appointed day.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To take away, to deprive (Kangxi: 剝也), to reduce.
  2. Used in 刻薄, 苛刻 (harsh) (Kangxi: 刻薄慘覈也).
  3. To injure (Kangxi: 害也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【刻】 (khok)
    • (verb-like) [to] engrave, [to] cut
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] injure
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] appoint
    • (adjective-like) (met.) harsh
    • (noun-like) [time]-mark

剋 Hak꜆ (hak1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼑ + 7
Unicode code point
U+524B
Alternative forms
  • 克
  • 勀
  • 勊
  • 尅
Reading variations
  • haak1 (longer syllable)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 33 尅) 殺也損也又五行生尅
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 克꜆; here normalised to 剋)
    • (…);
    • overbearing;
    • (…).
  • (Page 70 尅꜆ Hák꜆ (haak1); here normalised to 剋 Hak꜆ (hak1))
    • (…);
    • to repress, to exorcise;
    • urgent (Kangxi 剋: 必也急也);
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ shát꜆ ꜀hung ꜁shan (hak1 saat3 hung1 san4 剋煞凶神), to drive off unlucky demons;
    • ꜀shang hák꜆ hak꜆ (sang1 hak1 生剋) to produce and destroy (said of the five elements);
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ chai꜄ (hak1 zai3 剋制) to lord it over one;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ ꜂kám (hak1 gaam2 剋減), to reduce a debt or wages by force;
    • hák꜆ hak꜆ pok꜇ (hak1 bok6 剋薄) hardfisted, oppressive, insulting.

    (Alternative form of 刻 (for all senses except the time-unit sense). See 刻 hak1.)

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 克. See 克 hak1 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 剋薄: Now usually spelt 刻薄.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To appoint (a date) (Kangxi: 剋期約定日期也).
  2. (astrology) To conflict with, to be harmful to.

黑 Hak꜆ (hak1)

Radical + residual strokes
⿊ + 0
Unicode code point
U+9ED1
Reading variations
  • haak1 (longer syllable)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 33 黑) 北方之色
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 黑꜆)
    • Black (Kangxi: 火所熏之色也, 北方隂色), sooty;
    • dark, obscure (Kangxi: 黑晦也如晦冥時色也);
    • cloudy, dull;
    • wicked;
    • hak꜆ shik꜆ (hak1 sik1 黑色) black color;
    • hak꜆ òm꜄ (hak1 am3 黑暗) dark, not well lighted;
    • hak꜆ ꜀sam (hak1 sam1 黑心), villainous;
    • hak꜆ fo꜄ (hak1 fo3 黑貨) opium;
    • hak꜆ pák꜇ ꜁nán ꜀fan (hak1 baak6 naan4 fan1 黑白難分), good and bad are not easily distinguished;
    • ꜀kam ꜃mán hak꜆ (gam1 maan5-1 hak1 今晚黑) to-night.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 黑貨: More generally, contraband.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. The 203rd radical (U+2FCA ⿊ "black").
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【黑】 (xok)
    • (adjective-like) black
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【黑麻麻】 (hak1 maa4-1 maa4-1)
    1. Very dark (of viewing conditions).
  • 【黑𠵼𠵼】 (hak1 mang1 mang1)
    1. Very dark (of viewing conditions, or in colour).
  • 【黑咪𠵼】 (hak1 mi1 mang1)
    1. Synonym of 黑𠵼𠵼.
  • 【黑屎竻掘】 (hak1 si2 lak6 gwak6)
    1. Black or very dark in colour (when undesirable).

The spellings 𠵼𠵼 for mang1 mang1, 咪𠵼 for mi1 mang1, and 竻掘 for lak6 gwak6 are arbitrary; these are likely phonetic suffixes.
