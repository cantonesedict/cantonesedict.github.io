Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 718 Kʻak꜆ (kak1) exemplified; here assigned to 揢)

    A colloquial word.

    • Uneven, interrupted;
    • lak꜆ kʻak꜆ (lak1 kak1 肋揢) difficult, not smooth;
    • lò꜅ ꜂hò lak꜆ kʻak꜆ (lou6 hou2 lak1 kak1 路好肋揢) the road is very rough;
    • ꜂kong tak꜆ lak꜆ lak꜆ kʻak꜆ kʻak꜆ (gong2 dak1 lak1 lak1 kak1 kak1 講得肋肋揢揢) to stammer, to hesitate.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【肋揢】 (lak1 kak1)
    1. Unsmooth, stuttering.

The spelling 肋揢 is arbitrary.

Wiktionary as of 2026-05-31 suggests 犖确 "rocky/uneven" (whose expected reading is lok6 hok6) as a possible etymology. On the other hand, I am more inclined to believe that 揢 kak1 is cognate with 撠 kik1 (TODO).
