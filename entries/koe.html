Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 187 #3: ꜀Kʻù (koe1).; here assigned to 𢳉)
    • To roll up in a ball;
    • to rumple;
    • to rub between the hands,;
    • ꜀kʻù ꜃ün (koe1 jyun5 𢳉撋), to roll up in a ball;
    • mok꜇ ꜀kʻù ꜁chʻáu (mok6 koe1 caau4 莫𢳉巢), don't rumple it;
    • mat꜆ ꜀kʻù kòm꜄ lün꜄ lün꜅ (mat1 koe1 gam3 lyun6 乜𢳉咁亂) why is it so tangled?
    • ꜀kʻù lù꜅ lù꜄ (koe1 loe3 𢳉𢶤) improperly, troublesome.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 乜𢳉咁亂: Corrected lün꜄ (lyun3) to lün꜅ (lyun6).
  • Williams' 𢳉𢶤: Corrected lù꜅ (loe6) to lù꜄ (loe3).
