墾 ꜂Han (han2)

Radical + residual strokes
⼟ + 13
Unicode code point
U+58BE
Alternative forms
  • 垦 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 83 墾) 開墾又[治]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 ꜂墾)
    • To open new land (Kangxi: 一曰開田用力反土也), to plough, to cultivate (Kangxi: 力治也);
    • to injure (Kangxi: 傷也如鉏墾之傷物也);
    • energetic;
    • ꜀hoi ꜂han (hoi1 han2 開墾), to clear land;
    • ꜂han tí꜅ (han2 dei6 墾地) new land;
    • ꜂han chung꜄ (han2 zung3 墾種) to plough and sow.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected 冶 to 治.
  • Williams' "to injure": Better rendered "to damage".
  • Williams' "energetic": This is a misreading of 力 in the various Kangxi definitions.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【墾】 (khonX)
    • (verb-like) [to] plough up, [to] open up
    • (verb-like) [to] damage

懇 ꜂Han (han2)

Radical + residual strokes
⼼ + 13
Unicode code point
U+61C7
Alternative forms
  • 豤
  • 貇
  • 頎
  • 恳 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 83 懇) 情切也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 ꜂懇)
    • To request, to beg, to ask as a favor;
    • importunate, truly, earnestly (Kangxi: 悃也, 誠也, 信也, 或作貇, 款誠之意);
    • ꜂han ꜁kʻau (han2 kau4 懇求), to intreat;
    • ꜂han kʻap꜆ (han2 kap1 懇給) ask to give;
    • ꜂han ꜀yan (han2 jan1 懇恩), to ask a kindness.
Conway's commentary
  • Kangxi 亦作頎《禮檀弓》頎乎其至也《註》頎音懇惻隱之貌 is difficult to make sense of. On the surface, this appears to define 頎 (and 懇) as "pitying". But that does not make sense; the context is 稽顙而後拜頎乎其至也, in which 頎乎其至也 describes a mourner kowtowing them bowing to a visitor. The mourner can hardly be "pitying" the visitor. The only way out of this conundrum is if 惻隱之貌 is "pitiable", but that interpretation is still not very satisfactory.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【懇】 (khonX)
    • (adjective-like) sincere

꜁Han (han4 陽平)

  • 痕

痕 ꜁Han (han4)

Radical + residual strokes
⽧ + 6
Unicode code point
U+75D5
Reading variations
  • han4-2 (vernacular rising-tone noun for line-shaped "mark" sense and rising second in vernacular 痕痕地)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 82 痕) [癢]痕又水痕
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 ꜁痕)
    • A scar, a cicatrix (Kangxi: 胝瘢也, 瘢痕也);
    • a mark, trace, or line left (Kangxi: 凡物有迹者皆曰痕 etc.);
    • an itching;
    • lui꜅ ꜁han (leoi6 han4 淚痕), marks of tears;
    • ꜂shui ꜁han (seoi2 han4 水痕), a stain from water;
    • ꜂hò ꜁han (hou2 han4 好痕), very itchy;
    • ꜁han tsik꜆ (han4 zik1 痕跡) a trace, a flaw in glass.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected malformed 癢.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【痕】 (hon)
    • (noun-like) scar
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【痕】 (han4)
    1. Itchy.
      • 痕癢 (han4 joeng5): "Itchiness."
      • 𢯎痕 (ngaau1 han4): "Scratch [an] itch."
      • 痕痕地 (han4 han4-2 dei6-2): "[A] bit itchy."
      • 屎窟痕 (si2 fat1 han4): "[His] arse [must have been] itchy" (i.e. he had no good reason to do that).

The spelling 痕 is arbitrary (but well established).
