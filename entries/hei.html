Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 78 ꜀Hé (he1) exemplified; here assigned to 唏 and hypothesised to be modern hei1)
    • A colloquial exclamation, expressing disapproval;
    • ꜀hé ꜁'m ꜂hò tʻau꜄ tau꜄ (hei1, m4 hou2 dau3 唏唔好鬥) do n't don't touch it!
Conway's commentary
  • Strict analogy with Williams' ꜀hé results in the modern reading he1. However, because Williams' ꜀hé was likely sounded *[heɪ] (based on his description of the final é), I hypothesise here that ꜀hé actually corresponds to the modern interjection hei1, which is sounded [hei].
  • Williams' 唏唔好鬥: Corrected tʻau꜄ (tau3) to tau꜄ (dau3).
