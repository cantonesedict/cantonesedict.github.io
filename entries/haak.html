Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 33 赫 Hak꜆ (hak1)) 明也盛也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 赫꜆ Hak꜆ (hak1); here normalised to Hák꜆ (haak1))
    • Bright red, splendid, luminous, like a fire or the sun (Kangxi: 火赤貌 etc., 赫赫然盛也, 赫顯也);
    • angry (Kangxi: 赫怒意);
    • to scorch (Kangxi: 赫炙也), to glisten;
    • clever, elegant;
    • hak꜆ hák꜆ hak꜆ hák꜆ (haak1 haak1 赫赫) hot, glorious, effulgent;
    • ꜂hín hak꜆ hák꜆ (hin2 haak1 顯赫) a good scholar, talented.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' "to scorch": Kangxi has
    又《集韻》虛訝切音罅與嚇同《詩大雅》反予來赫《傳》赫炙也《箋》口距人謂之赫《釋文》毛許白反鄭許嫁反莊子所云以梁國嚇我是也
    Unravelling this, we have competing interpretations:
    1. From the commentary, 赫 is to be read 許白反 hák꜆ (haak1), and means "scorching" or "fieriness".
    2. From the lesser-note, 赫 is to be read 許嫁反 há꜄ (haa3) and is being used for 嚇 "menace".
    Whilst both meanings are reasonable, the departing-tone reading is indefensible because rhyme is being made with 時亦弋獲. Thus, if one is to interpret 赫 as meaning "menace", then it must be read hák꜆ (haak3) and not há꜄ (haa3).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. A surname (Kangxi: 姓).
  2. Used in 赫連 (a compound surname) (Kangxi: 赫連氏複姓).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【赫】 (xaek)
    • (adjective-like) fiery-red, fiery
    • (adjective-like) (met.) resplendent
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] resplend

嚇 Hák꜆ (haak3)

Radical + residual strokes

U+5687

  • 哧
  • 赫
  • 吓 (Mainland simplified)
Reading variations

Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 90 嚇) [恐]嚇[驚]嚇
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 嚇꜆)
    • An angry tone sound (Kangxi: 怒其聲);
    • to threaten, to intimidate;
    • to anger;
    • scared, frightened;
    • hák꜆ ꜃ngo yat꜆ tʻiú꜄ (haak3 ngo5 jat1 tiu3 嚇我一跳) scared me much;
    • hák꜆ ꜀keng (haak3 geng1 嚇驚), terrified;
    • hák꜆ ꜀tsʻan (haak3 can1 嚇嚫), sick from fright;
    • hák꜆ chá꜄ (haak3 zaa3 嚇詐) to alarm by threats;
    • hák꜆ shai꜄ (haak3 sai3 嚇勢) a humbug;
    • ꜂hung hák꜆ (hung2 haak3 恐嚇) to idly arouse one's fears.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed incomplete and missing strokes in 恐 and 驚.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To defy verbally (Kangxi: 口拒人謂之赫, 赫亦作嚇).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【嚇】 (xaek, xaeH)
    • (verb-like) [to] frighten
    • (verb-like) [to] menace
