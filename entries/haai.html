꜀Hái (haai1 陰平)

  • 揩

揩 ꜀Hái (haai1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼿ + 9
Unicode code point
U+63E9
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 25 揩) [虛佳切擦]也[磨]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 ꜀揩)
    • To rub, to wipe (Kangxi: 摩拭也);
    • ꜀hái ꜀kon tseng꜅ (haai1 gon1 zeng6 揩乾淨) wipe [shoes] clean;
    • ꜀hái ꜁mo (haai1 mo4 揩磨), to scour off (as rust);
    • ꜀hái ꜁mái ꜁tsʻéung (haai1 maai4 coeng4 揩埋牆) rub it on the wall;
    • ꜃ní mok꜇ ꜀hái ꜀tsʻan ꜃ngo (nei5 mok6 haai1 can1 ngo5 你莫揩嚫我), don't rub against me;
    • ꜀hái tsʻat꜆ tsʻát꜆ (haai1 caat3 揩擦) to rub (as with sandpaper).
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected ⿸虍皿 to 虛, corrected malformed 佳切擦, and fixed missing final stroke in 磨.
  • Williams' 揩擦: Corrected tsʻat꜆ (cat1) to tsʻát꜆ (caat3).
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【揩】 (haai1)
    1. To brush against.
      • 揩到佢架車 (haai1 dou3-2 keoi5 gaa3 ce1): "Brushed against his car."
      • 揩花咗 (haai1 faa1 zo2): "Scratched [from being] brushed against."

嚡 ꜁Hái (haai4)

Radical + residual strokes
⼝ + 15
Unicode code point
U+56A1
Alternative forms
  • 鞋 (for Cantonese "rough" sense)
Reading variations
  • haai4-2 (rising second in vernacular 嚡嚡地)
  • haai5 (rising alternative for interjection)
Fan Wan entry
(Not present)
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 ꜁嚡)
    • An exclamation of disappointment, Alas! Oh!
    • rough, hispid, harsh;
    • lak꜇ kòm꜄ ꜁hái (lak6-1 gam3 haai4 竻咁嚡), sharp as a bramble, morose, surly;
    • ꜀tsʻò ꜁hái (cou1 haai4 粗嚡), coarse and rough;
    • ꜃kʻü sham꜅ ꜁hái (keoi5 sam6 haai4 佢甚嚡), he's very stingy.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【嚡】 (haai4, haai5)
    1. An interjection of disappointment, complaint, etc. (i.e. a sigh).
  • 【嚡】 (haai4)
    1. Rough (of a surface or texture).
      • 煮嚡咗 (zyu2 haai4 zo2): "Cooked [it] rough" (i.e. overcooked, as opposed to 滑 "smooth").
  • 【嚡澀澀】 (haai4 saap6 saap6)
    1. Rough (of a surface or texture).

The spelling 嚡 is arbitrary both for the interjection and for "rough" (which are distinct words).

諧 ꜁Hái (haai4)

Radical + residual strokes
⾔ + 9
Unicode code point
U+8AE7
Alternative forms
  • 龤
  • 谐 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 26 諧) 和也合也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 ꜁諧)
    • To harmonize, to agree (Kangxi: 詥也);
    • to pair (Kangxi: 耦也);
    • consenting, in concord (Kangxi: 合也調也);
    • of one mind;
    • to laugh laughworthy, humorous (Kangxi: 諧之言皆也辭淺會俗皆悅笑也);
    • ꜁hái ꜃lò (haai4 lou5 諧老), an old couple;
    • ꜁wo ꜁hái (wo4 haai4 和諧), agreeing (as married people), cordial;
    • ꜁hái ꜀yam (haai4 jam1 諧音), a chord.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 和諧: In 21st-century usage, a euphemism for Internet censorship in China.
  • Williams' 諧音: Also near-homophony.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【諧】 (heaj)
    • (adjective-like) concordant, resonant

鞋 ꜁Hái (haai4)

Radical + residual strokes
⾰ + 6
Unicode code point
U+978B
Alternative forms
  • 𩋔
  • 𩋘
  • 鞵
Reading variations

Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 26 𩋘) 履𩋘
  • (Vol. 2 Image 26 鞋) 同上
  • (Vol. 2 Image 26 鞵) 同上
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 ꜁鞋)
    • A shoe (Kangxi: 鞋解也著時縮其上如履然解其上則舒解也, 本作鞵);
    • yat꜆ túi꜄ ꜁hái (jat1 deoi3 haai4 一對鞋), a pair of shoes;
    • ꜁hái hün꜄ (haai4 hyun3 鞋楦) a last;
    • ꜀kung ꜁hái (gung1 haai4 弓鞋), a woman's shoe;
    • ꜀tʻo ꜁hái (to1 haai4-2 拖鞋), slippers;
    • ꜁hái ꜀kam (haai4 gam1 鞋金), fees paid house agents;
    • ꜁hái pat꜇ (haai4 bat6 鞋拔) a shoe-horn;
    • ꜁shing ꜂tsai ꜁hái (sing4-2 zai2 haai4 繩仔鞋), cord sandals;
    • ꜂pò ꜁hái ꜂lò (bou2 haai4 lou2 補鞋佬) a cobbler;
    • chéuk꜆ ꜁hái (zoek3 haai4 着鞋), to put on shoes.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 嚡 (for the Cantonese "rough" sense). See 嚡 haai4 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 弓鞋: Per the practice of foot binding.
  • Williams' 鞋拔: The preferred term for "shoehorn" in Cantonese is 鞋抽.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【鞋】 (hea)
    • (noun-like) shoe

邂 ꜃Hái (haai5)

Radical + residual strokes
⾡ + 13
Unicode code point
U+9082
Alternative forms
  • 解
  • 迦
Reading variations
  • haai6 (per 《廣韻》胡懈切)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 27 邂) 邂逅不期而[會]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 ꜃邂)
    • Accidentally (Kangxi: 邂逅不期而會也), a pleasant hap;
    • ꜃hái hau꜅ (haai5 hau6 邂逅) an unexpected meeting.
Conway's commentary
  • The reading ꜃hái (haai5) is not unattested for the "carefree" sense (supplemented below); 《集韻》 has the reading 下買切, explained 邂逅解説皃.
  • Fan Wan and Williams print the component 牛 as 㐄.
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed third stroke in 會.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Carefree (Kangxi: 邂逅[解]悅貌).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【邂】 (heaH)
    • (adjective-like) carefree
    • Used in 邂逅.
  • 【邂逅】 (heaH huwH)
    • (adjective-like) carefree [and] pleased

懈 Hái꜅ (haai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⼼ + 13
Unicode code point
U+61C8
Alternative forms
  • 解
Reading variations
  • gaai3 (per 《廣韻》古隘切)
  • haai5 (uncommon)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 28 懈) 怠也倦也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 懈꜅)
    • Remiss, slow, negligent, inattentive (Kangxi: 怠也, 又或作解);
    • hái꜅ toi꜅ (haai6 doi6 懈怠) lazy, slow;
    • hái꜅ to꜅ (haai6 do6 懈惰) lazy.
Conway's commentary
  • The reading hái꜅ (haai6) is not unattested, though Kangxi considers it vulgar and erroneous (俗讀匣母非).
  • Fan Wan and Williams print the component 牛 as 㐄.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Tired (Fan Wan: 倦也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【解】 (heaX)
    • (adjective-like) lax, remiss
    • (verb-like) [to] laxen

械 Hái꜅ (haai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⽊ + 7
Unicode code point
U+68B0
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 28 械) 器械機械又[桎]梏也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 械꜅)
    • Military weapons, as spear, bow, &c. (Kangxi: 一曰器之總名);
    • shackles, fetters (Kangxi: 一曰桎梏也);
    • ꜀kí hái꜅ (gei1 haai6 機械) a curious contrivance, the nicer parts of a machine, a craft or art;
    • ꜀tsʻéung ꜀tò hí꜄ hái꜅ (coeng1 dou1 hei3 haai6 槍刀器械) guns spears, swords, and other arms.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected malformed 桎.
  • Williams' 槍刀器械: Unlikely to be "guns" unless the context is the Opium Wars.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. A utensil, implement, or mechanism.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【械】 (heajH)
    • (noun-like) shackle
    • (noun-like) weapon
    • (noun-like) utensil, mechanism

齘 Hái꜅ (haai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⿒ + 4
Unicode code point
U+9F58
Alternative forms
  • 𬹼 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 28 齘) 齒相[抵]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 69 齘꜅)
    • To gnash the teeth (Kangxi: 齒相切也), angry (Kangxi: 䫴齘者切齒怒也 etc.);
    • plates in mail arranged like teeth.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected ⿰扌氏 to 抵.
  • Williams' "plates in mail arranged like teeth": This is a misreading of Kangxi's example 凡甲衣之欲其無齘也, which should be rendered "For all armour: wearing it, [we] wish [that] it have not gnashing".
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【齘】 (heajH)
    • (verb-like) [to] gnash
