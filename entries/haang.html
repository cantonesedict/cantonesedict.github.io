Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 88 坑) 坑坎阱也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 74 ꜀坑)
    • A ditch, trench, hollow, hole, cave, or pit, natural or artificial (Kangxi: 同阬, 虛也, 陷也);
    • the furrows in a roof;
    • a pit to entrap wild beasts;
    • to throw into a pit;
    • to excavate;
    • ꜀háng ꜁kʻü (haang1 keoi4 坑渠), a sewer or drain;
    • ꜀háng ꜂hòm (haang1 ham2 坑坎), a natural gully, a ravine;
    • ꜂Lò ꜃Lò ꜀háng (lou5 haang1 老坑), a quarry in Sháuking Cháuking fú (肇慶府) which furnishes good inkstones;
    • ꜂shui ꜀háng ꜁mún (seoi2 haang1 mun4 水坑門), a sluice;
    • ꜁múi ꜀háng (mui4 haang1 煤坑), a coal pit.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 老坑: Corrected ꜂Lò (lou2) to ꜃Lò (lou5).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【阬】 (khaeng)
    • (noun-like) pit
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] bury
