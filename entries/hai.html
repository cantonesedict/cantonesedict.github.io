Conway's Cantonese entry

One of the five profanities censored on Hong Kong television.

  • 【閪】 (hai1)
    1. (vulgar) The vulva.
    2. (vulgar) The vagina.

    More or less equivalent to English "cunt". For non-literal senses, see the Wiktionary entry for 閪.

The spelling 閪 is arbitrary.

꜂Hai (hai2 陰上)

  • 喺

喺 ꜂Hai (hai2)

Radical + residual strokes
⼝ + 9
Unicode code point
U+55BA
Fan Wan entry
(Not present)
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 68 ꜂喺)
    • To be at, to be in;
    • is;
    • well so, no more;
    • a final particle denoting that there is no more to be said or done;
    • ꜃ní chung꜅ ꜂hai ko꜄ ꜂ko chʻü꜄ (nei5 zung6 hai2 go2 cyu3 你重喺嗰處) do you still stay there?
    • ꜂hai chü꜄ chʻü꜄ ꜁'m ꜂hai (hai2 cyu3 m4 hai2 喺處唔喺), is it there? is he in?
    • ꜂hai ꜀pín chʻü꜄ ꜁loi (hai2 bin1 cyu3 loi4 喺邊處來), where do you come from?
    • ꜂kòm yéung꜅ ꜂hai lé꜄ (gam2 joeng6-2 hai2 le3 噉樣喺唎) that will do, no more;
    • ꜂hai lok꜆ (hai2 lok3 喺咯) that's the fact, there!—an expression of surprise.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' "is": This sense is incorrect.
  • Williams' "well so, no more", "a final particle […]": These senses are very dated.
  • Williams' 你重喺嗰處: Changed ko꜄ (go3) to modern ꜂ko (go2).
  • Williams' 喺處唔喺: Corrected chü꜄ (zyu3) to chʻü꜄ (cyu3). This is dated syntax. In modern Cantonese, it would be 喺唔喺處, with only the verb hugging the negative.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【喺】 (hai2)
    1. To be at, to be in.
      • 我喺屋企 (ngo5 hai2 uk1 kei2): "I'm at home."
      • 我喺街 (ngo5 hai2 gaai1): "I'm in [the] street" (i.e. out and about).
    2. At, in.
      • 喺澳洲 (hai2 ou3 zau1): "In Australia."

The spelling 喺 is arbitrary (but well established).

係 Hai꜅ (hai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⼈ + 7
Unicode code point
U+4FC2
Alternative forms
  • 系
Reading variations
  • gai3 (per 《廣韻》古詣切)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 34 係) 戀也又[聯]也是也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 68 係꜅)
    • The substantive verb;
    • to be, am, is;
    • to connect, to succeed to, to relate to (Kangxi: 係繼也);
    • ꜀pín hai꜅ (bin1 hai6 邊係) not at all so;
    • hai꜅ ꜁'m hai꜅ (hai6 m4 hai6 係唔係) is it so?
    • ꜀hán hai꜅ (haan1 hai6 ？係) was it not so?
    • hai꜅ lok꜇ lok꜆ (hai6 lok3 係咯) yes;
    • ꜁'m hai꜅ (m4 hai6 唔係) no;
    • ꜂tím hai꜅ (dim2 hai6 點係) how will it be?
    • ꜂tsung hai꜅ ꜂kòm (zung2 hai6 gam2 總係噉), it is all just so;
    • tsau꜅ hai꜅ ꜃kʻü (zau6 hai6 keoi5 就係佢), it is just him;
    • hai꜅ ꜁mo ꜀mo (hai6 mo1 係麼), is it not so?
    • hai꜅ ꜀tò ꜃yau (hai6 dou1 jau5 係都有), everything is here, you have all sorts;
    • ꜂kwo ꜁ín hai꜅ (gwo2 jin4 hai6 果然係) it is the case, you have all sorts;
    • chí꜄ hai꜅ (zi3 hai6 至係) at the time, that is better.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 繫. See 繫 hai6 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • The reading hai꜅ (hai6) is not unattested; 《集韻》 has the reading 胡計切, explained 説文絜[束]也通作結.
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected malformed 聯.
  • Williams' 係咯: Corrected lok꜇ (lok6) to lok꜆ (lok3). Better rendered "[it] is".
  • Williams' 唔係: Better rendered "[it] isn't".
  • Williams' 係麼: Corrected ꜁mo (mo4) to ꜀mo (mo1).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To tie, to bind (Kangxi: 係縛也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【係】 (kejH)
    • (verb-like) [to] tie, [to] bind
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] connect, [to] relate (to)
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【係】 (hai6)
    1. (copula) To be.
      • 係 (hai6): "[It] is."
      • 唔係 (m4 hai6): "[It] isn't."
      • 係咪 (hai6 mai6): "Is [it or] isn't [it]?"
      • 係咪啊 (hai6 mai6 aa3): "Is [it or] isn't [it]?" (i.e. am I not right?)
      • 係人都想 (hai6 jan4 dou1 soeng2): "All [that] are human want to" (i.e. everyone wants to).
      • 係得咁多 (hai6 dak1 gam3 do1): "[There] is only that much."
      • 係定啦 (hai6 ding6-2 laa1): "[It] certainly is!"
      • 又係佢 (jau6 hai6 keoi5): "[It] is him again."
      • 即係話 (zik1 hai6 waa6): "That is [to] say."
  • 【係咪】 (hai6 mai6)
    1. Short for 係唔係, "is [it or] isn't [it]".

The copulative sense of 係 is not exclusive to Cantonese. It appears in formal writing, for example:

  • In numerous entries of 《集韻考正》, typically in the stock comment 案此係新坿字, "Note. This is [a] New Annex character", made against characters not in the original 《說文》.
  • In Yu Ying-shih's graduation certificate, which begins with 學生余英時係安徽省市潛山縣人.

However, it is mainly Cantonese and Hakka that use it in everyday speech, leading to the (incorrect) perception that it is informal.

系 Hai꜅ (hai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⽷ + 1
Unicode code point
U+7CFB
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 34 系) 世系緒也[繼]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 68 系꜅)
    • To connect, to tie (Kangxi: 繫也, 相連繫也);
    • to continue (Kangxi: 系繼也);
    • a succession (Kangxi: 系者胤也);
    • shai꜄ hai꜅ (sai3 hai6 世系) a genealogy, a family record;
    • ꜀séung hai꜅ (soeng1 hai6 相系) continuous.

    (Intrinsically, and otherwise, an alternative form of 繫. See 繫 hai6 for the canonical entry.)

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 係. See 係 hai6 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected malformed 繼.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. A system.
  2. An educational department or faculty.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【系】 (hejH)
    • (verb-like) [to] connect
    • (noun-like) (met.) lineage

繫 Hai꜅ (hai6)

Radical + residual strokes
⽷ + 13
Unicode code point
U+7E6B
Alternative forms
  • 係
  • 傒
  • 系
Reading variations
  • gai3 (per 《廣韻》古詣切 for "tie/bind" sense)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 1 Image 31 [繫] Kai꜄ (gai3)) 結也束縛也
  • (Vol. 1 Image 34 繫) 因繫[羈]繫[又]音計
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 68 繫꜅)
    • To tie, to bind (Kangxi: 約束也);
    • to continue;
    • attached to (Kangxi: 繫辭者聖人繫屬此辭于卦爻之下), to think of;
    • to retain, to hold;
    • hai꜅ ꜀chau (hai6 zau1 繫舟), to fasten a boat;
    • hai꜅ ꜃lui lui꜅ (hai6 leoi6 繫累), to implicate.
  • (Page 120 繫꜄ Kai꜄ (gai3))
    • Usually read hai꜄ hai꜅ (hai6), to tie;
    • to pertain to, to follow in consequence of.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed incomplete stroke in (first) headword 繫, corrected malformed 羈, and fixed missing character 又.
  • Williams' "to continue": This is a misreading of Kangxi 續也 (but an understandable one, since 續也 is a very poor way of explaining the "connect" sense).
  • Williams' 繫累: Corrected ꜃lui (leoi5) to lui꜅ (leoi6).
  • Williams' "Usually read […]": Corrected hai꜄ (hai3) to hai꜅ (hai6).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To connect (Kangxi: 繫聯綴也).
  2. To suspend, to hang.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【繫】 (hejH)
    • (verb-like) [to] connect
    • (verb-like) [to] suspend, [to] hang, (met.) [to] attach
  • 【繫】 (kejH)
    • (verb-like) [to] tie, [to] bind
