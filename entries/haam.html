Radical + residual strokes

Alternative forms

Reading variations

Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 64 喊) 譁聲
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 喊꜄)
    • To call, to halloo after, to call for, to vociferate (Kangxi 嚂: 聲也亦與喊同);
    • a call (Kangxi: 喊聲也);
    • to cry, to bawl;
    • angry cries (Kangxi: 怒聲);
    • hám꜄ ꜃kʻü (haam3 keoi5 喊佢) call him;
    • tái꜅ ꜀sheng hám꜄ (daai6 seng1 haam3 大聲喊) to call aloud, to cry out;
    • hám꜄ huk꜆ (haam3 huk1 喊哭) to cry;
    • ꜂tá hám꜄ lò꜅ (daa2 haam3 lou6 打喊露) to gape;
    • hám꜄ ꜀tʻung tʻau꜄ (haam3 tung1 tau3 喊通透) to cry it out;
    • hám꜄ shát꜆ ꜁lín ꜀tʻín (haam3 saat3 lin4 tin1 喊殺連天), the noise of battle cries reaches to heaven;
    • hám꜄ ꜀sheng tái꜅ ké꜄ (haam3 seng1 daai6 ge3 喊聲大嘅) useless regrets;
    • hám꜄ kau꜄ (haam3 gau3 喊救) to call upon to save.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' 打喊露: Better rendered "to yawn".
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【嚂】 (xamX)
    • (verb-like) [to] cry (out)
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【打喊露】 (daa2 haam3 lou6)
    1. To yawn.
  • 【喊】 (haam3)
    1. To cry, to shed tears.

函 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⼐ + 6
Unicode code point
U+51FD
Han unification vigilance
May appear with central component as 了 (assumed here) or 丂, depending on the font.
Alternative forms
  • 凾
  • 圅
  • 椷 (for "contain" sense)
  • 䤴 (for "armour" sense)
  • 䥁 (for "armour" sense)
Reading variations
  • ham4 (per 《廣韻》胡男切 for "contain" sense)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 函) 包也[容]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁函)
    • To contain, to infold, to comprehend in, to envelop (Kangxi: 容也, 函包也, 讀與含同);
    • a letter, or what is contained in an envelop (Kangxi: 書也);
    • liberal, capacious;
    • ꜀páu ꜁hám (baau1 haam4 包函), to keep close, not to utter;
    • ꜀shü ꜁hám (syu1 haam4 書函), a letter;
    • ꜀tsün ꜁hám (zyun1 haam4 尊函), your favor;
    • ꜁hám chéung꜅ (haam4 zoeng6 函丈) a teacher or sage's desk;
    • ꜁hám ꜁yung (haam4 jung4 函容), patiently.
Conway's commentary
  • The "contain" sense is probably cognate with 含.
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing second stroke in 容.
  • Williams' 尊函: Here, "favor" means "letter".
  • Williams' 函丈: Not a teacher's desk, but a teacher, from the distance prescribed by the Record of Rites, 席間函丈 "between [the] mats [there shall be] contained [a] measure".
  • In modern usage, used in 函數 "function" in mathematics.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Armour (Kangxi: 鎧也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【函】 (hom)
    • (verb-like) [to] contain
    • (noun-like) container, (met.) armour

咸 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⼝ + 6
Unicode code point
U+54B8
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 咸) 皆也又[交感]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁咸)
    • All, jointly, altogether (Kangxi: 皆也, 悉也);
    • completely, totally;
    • always, all round, reaching everywhere (Kangxi: 咸徧也);
    • concord (Kangxi: 咸同也), associated;
    • hasty;
    • a diagram (Kangxi: 卦名);
    • pat꜆ ꜁hám (bat1 haam4 不咸), disagreeing;
    • ꜁Hám ꜀fung (haam4 fung1 咸豐), the style of the present emperor;
    • mán꜅ kwok꜆ ꜁hám ꜁ning (maan6 gwok3 haam4 ning4 萬國咸寧), all countries at peace.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 諴. See 諴 haam4 for the canonical entry.)

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 鹹. See 鹹 haam4 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected ⿳一八夂 to 交, and fixed incompletely printed stroke in 感.
  • Williams' "hasty": Kangxi has 《易雜卦》咸速也. This should not be taken as a definition of 咸.
  • Williams' 咸豐: See the term 咸豐年 below.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. A surname (Kangxi: 姓).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【咸】 (heam)
    • (adverb-like) all, completely, together
    • (verb-like) [to] unite
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【咸豐年】 (haam4 fung1 nin4)
    1. Ages ago.

Note that Emperor Hien-fung (咸豐) reigned from 1850 to 1861, and died at the age of just 30.

涵 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⽔ + 8
Unicode code point
U+6DB5
Reading variations
  • ham4 (per 《廣韻》胡男切)
  • ham6 (per 《廣韻》胡感切 for "boat-leak" sense)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 涵) 水澤涵[濡]
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁涵)
    • Submerged (Kangxi: 涵沉也);
    • to leak (Kangxi: 一曰水入船);
    • marshy;
    • to soak;
    • to contain (Kangxi: 容也), capacious;
    • ꜁hám ꜁ün ꜃ün (haam4 jyun5 涵軟), to soak soft;
    • ꜁hám ꜃yéung (haam4 joeng5 涵養), kindly, to keep one's temper;
    • ꜂shui ꜁hám (seoi2 haam4 水涵) a sewer.
Conway's commentary
  • The reading ꜁hám (haam4) is not unattested; 《集韻》 has the reading 胡讒切, explained 同也詩僭始既涵鄭康成讀.
  • I fail to see why the "contain" sense of 涵 is not considered the same word as 函 (which is probably cognate with 含).
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing strokes in 濡.
  • Williams' "marshy": This is a misreading of Kangxi 水澤多也. Here, 澤 means "moistened".
  • Williams' 涵軟: Corrected ꜁ün (jyun4) to ꜃ün (jyun5).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Well-moistened (Kangxi: 水澤多也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【涵】 (hom)
    • (verb-like) [to] contain
    • (adjective-like) well-moistened, well-soaked

Radical + residual strokes

Alternative forms

Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 啣) 口中物
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 銜) 馬勒也又口[含物]曰銜又官之階位曰銜
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 ꜁銜)
    • Bit of a bridle (Kangxi: 馬勒口中, 馬銜所以制之行也);
    • to hold in the mouth (Kangxi: 凡口含物曰銜);
    • to contain;
    • to control one's self, to guide, to order;
    • affected by;
    • moved thankful (Kangxi: 感也), indignant resentful (Kangxi: 憾也);
    • rank (Kangxi: 官吏階位曰銜);
    • ꜂hau ꜁hám muk꜇ ꜂lám (hau2 haam4 muk6 laam5-2 口銜木欖), to keep silence;
    • ꜁hám ꜁múi (haam4 mui4 銜枚), to hold a bit, to be still;
    • ꜀kún ꜁hám (gun1 haam4 官銜) or chik꜆ ꜁hám (zik1 haam4 職銜), an official title;
    • ꜁hám han꜅ (haam4 han6 銜恨) to restrain one's indignation.
  • (Page 71 ꜁？꜁㘅; here normalised to 銜)
    • To hold in the mouth;
    • used for the preceding (銜) (Kangxi 㘅: 同銜);
    • to receive (as an order);
    • ꜁hám ꜁wán (haam4 waan4 銜環), to hold a gem in the bill, met. to requite a kindness;
    • ꜁hám tʻít꜆ ꜁tʻán tán꜅ (haam4 tit3 daan6-2 銜鐵彈), to hold an iron shot in the mouth (punishment of liars in hell);
    • ꜁hám pò꜄ (haam4 bou3 銜報) to requite;
    • ꜁hám meng꜅ ming꜅ (haam4 ming6 銜命) received your orders (said of a friend's request);
    • ꜁hám chü꜅ ꜁tʻong ꜂lám (haam4 zyu6 tong4 laam5-2 銜住糖欖) sucking a sugared olive, met. pleased and silent.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected both occurrences of 𢪱 to 物, and corrected 舍 to 含.
  • Williams prints the first mouth-radical headword as ⿰口䣃 (rather than 啣). Yet another form, 𠷢, appears on Page 748 in the character index.
  • Williams' 銜鐵彈: Corrected ꜁tʻán (taan4) to tán꜅ (daan6-2).
  • Williams' 銜命: Corrected meng꜅ (meng6) to ming꜅ (ming6).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. To carry (out) (Kangxi: 奉君命而行曰銜命).
  2. To connect.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【銜】 (haem)
    • (noun-like) bit
    • (verb-like) [to] carry in [the] mouth, [to] carry

餡 Hám꜅ (haam6)

Radical + residual strokes
⾷ + 8
Unicode code point
U+9921
Han unification vigilance
May appear with radical component 𩙿 or 飠 (assumed here), depending on the font.
Alternative forms
  • 膁
  • 䭑
  • 馅 (Mainland simplified)
Reading variations
  • haam6-2 (vernacular rising-tone noun)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 65 [餡]) 餅中之[物]
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 71 餡꜅)
    • Small flour cakes, like dumplings, with meat or fruit inside The filling within flour cakes, dumplings, &c. (Kangxi: 凡米麫食物坎其中實以雜味曰餡或作䭑);
    • ꜂páu ꜀páu hám꜅ (baau1 haam6-2 包餡) pastry cakes cake filling;
    • ꜁wan ꜀tʻan hám꜅ (wan4 tan1 haam6-2 雲吞餡) the filling of pork dumplings cooked in sugar.
Conway's commentary
  • The original departing reading haam6 is almost never heard, having been supplanted by vernacular rising haam6-2.
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected headword ⿰𩙿⿱⺈曰 to 餡, and corrected 𢪱 to 物.
  • Williams' "Small flour cakes, like dumplings, […]": This is a misreading of Kangxi's definition. 餡 refers to the filling, not to the entire bun or dumpling.
  • Williams' 包餡: Corrected ꜂páu (baau2) to ꜀páu (baau1).
  • Williams' 雲吞餡: Evidently different from the modern wonton, which is not cooked in sugar.
