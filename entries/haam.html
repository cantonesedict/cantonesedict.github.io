函 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⼐ + 6
Unicode code point
U+51FD
Han unification vigilance
May appear with central component as 了 (assumed here) or 丂, depending on the font.
Alternative forms
  • 凾
  • 圅
  • 䤴 (for "armour" sense)
  • 䥁 (for "armour" sense)
Reading variations
  • ham4 (per 《廣韻》胡男切 for "contain" sense)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 函) 包也[容]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁函)
    • To contain, to infold, to comprehend in, to envelop (Kangxi: 容也, 函包也, 讀與含同);
    • a letter, or what is contained in an envelop (Kangxi: 書也);
    • liberal, capacious;
    • ꜀páu ꜁hám (baau1 haam4 包函), to keep close, not to utter;
    • ꜀shü ꜁hám (syu1 haam4 書函), a letter;
    • ꜀tsün ꜁hám (zyun1 haam4 尊函), your favor;
    • ꜁hám chéung꜅ (haam4 zoeng6 函丈) a teacher or sage's desk;
    • ꜁hám ꜁yung (haam4 jung4 函容), patiently.
Conway's commentary
  • The "contain" sense is probably cognate with 含.
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing second stroke in 容.
  • Williams' 尊函: Here, "favor" means "letter".
  • Williams' 函丈: Not a teacher's desk, but a teacher, from the distance prescribed by the Record of Rites, 席間函丈 "between [the] mats [there shall be] contained [a] measure".
  • In modern usage, used in 函數 "function" in mathematics.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Armour (Kangxi: 鎧也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【函】 (hom)
    • (verb-like) [to] contain
    • (noun-like) container, (met.) armour

咸 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⼝ + 6
Unicode code point
U+54B8
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 咸) 皆也又[交感]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁咸)
    • All, jointly, altogether (Kangxi: 皆也, 悉也);
    • completely, totally;
    • always, all round, reaching everywhere (Kangxi: 咸徧也);
    • concord (Kangxi: 咸同也), associated;
    • hasty;
    • a diagram (Kangxi: 卦名);
    • pat꜆ ꜁hám (bat1 haam4 不咸), disagreeing;
    • ꜁Hám ꜀fung (haam4 fung1 咸豐), the style of the present emperor;
    • mán꜅ kwok꜆ ꜁hám ꜁ning (maan6 gwok3 haam4 ning4 萬國咸寧), all countries at peace.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 諴. See 諴 haam4 for the canonical entry.) (TODO)

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 鹹. See 鹹 haam4 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected ⿳一八夂 to 交, and fixed incompletely printed stroke in 感.
  • Williams' "hasty": Kangxi has 《易雜卦》咸速也. This should not be taken as a definition of 咸.
  • Williams' 咸豐: See the term 咸豐年 below.
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. A surname (Kangxi: 姓).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【咸】 (heam)
    • (adverb-like) all, completely, together
    • (verb-like) [to] unite
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【咸豐年】 (haam4 fung1 nin4)
    1. Ages ago.

Note that Emperor Hien-fung (咸豐) reigned from 1850 to 1861, and died at the age of just 30.

涵 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⽔ + 8
Unicode code point
U+6DB5
Reading variations
  • ham4 (per 《廣韻》胡男切)
  • ham6 (per 《廣韻》胡感切 for "boat-leak" sense)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 涵) 水澤涵[濡]
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁涵)
    • Submerged (Kangxi: 涵沉也);
    • to leak (Kangxi: 一曰水入船);
    • marshy;
    • to soak;
    • to contain (Kangxi: 容也), capacious;
    • ꜁hám ꜁ün ꜃ün (haam4 jyun5 涵軟), to soak soft;
    • ꜁hám ꜃yéung (haam4 joeng5 涵養), kindly, to keep one's temper;
    • ꜂shui ꜁hám (seoi2 haam4 水涵) a sewer.
Conway's commentary
  • The reading ꜁hám (haam4) is not unattested; 《集韻》 has the reading 胡讒切, explained 同也詩僭始既涵鄭康成讀.
  • I fail to see why the "contain" sense of 涵 is not considered the same word as 函 (which is probably cognate with 含).
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing strokes in 濡.
  • Williams' "marshy": This is a misreading of Kangxi 水澤多也. Here, 澤 means "moistened".
  • Williams' 涵軟: Corrected ꜁ün (jyun4) to ꜃ün (jyun5).
Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Well-moistened (Kangxi: 水澤多也).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【涵】 (hom)
    • (verb-like) [to] contain
    • (adjective-like) well-moistened, well-soaked

？鹹 ꜁Hám (haam4)

Radical + residual strokes
⿄ + 9
Unicode code point
U+9E79
Alternative forms
  • 咸
  • 醎
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 [鹹]) [鹽]味不[淡]也
  • (Vol. 2 Image 63 [醎]) 同上
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 70 ꜁鹹)
    • Saltish, like sea-water Salty (Kangxi: 北方味也, 不淡, 鹽味);
    • salted, preserved in brine;
    • ꜁hám shap꜆ (haam4 sap1 鹹濕) salacious;
    • ꜁hám tsʻoi꜄ (haam4 coi3 鹹菜) sour-krout;
    • ꜁hám ꜁tʻò (haam4 tou4-2 鹹桃), salted peaches.
Conway's commentary
  • Williams' prints the headword with the last stroke of 鹵 missing; here corrected. (The character 鹹 appears correctly on Page 828 in the character index.)
  • Fan Wan entry: Corrected 巳 to 口 in the component 咸 of headwords 鹹 and 醎, and corrected malformed 鹽 and 淡.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【鹹】 (heam)
    • (adjective-like) salty
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【鹹】 (haam4)
    1. Lewd, pornographic, erotic.
      • 鹹片 (haam4 pin3-2): "Pornographic video."
  • 【鹹濕】 (haam4 sap1)
    1. Lewd, pornographic, erotic.
      • 好鹹濕 (hou2 haam4 sap1): "Very lewd" (or erotic).
      • 鹹濕鬼 (haam4 sap1 gwai2): "Pervert."

菡 ꜃Hám (haam5)

Radical + residual strokes
⾋ + 8
Unicode code point
U+83E1
Reading variations
  • aam2 (per Williams)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 64 菡 ꜂Ám (aam2) or ꜂Hám (haam2)) 菡萏荷花也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 4 ꜂菡 ꜂Ám (aam2); here normalised to ꜃Hám (haam5))
    • An unopened flower (Kangxi: 菡猶含也未吐之意);
    • ꜂ám ꜃hám tám꜅ (haam5 daam6 菡萏), the lotus flower;
    • a poetical name.
Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: The initial is uncertain.
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【菡】 (homX)
    • Used in 菡萏.
  • 【菡萏】 (homX domX)
    • (noun-like) lotus flower
