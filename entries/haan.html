Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜀慳)
    • Saving, niggardly, stingy;
    • to be sparing of;
    • ꜀hán kím꜅ (haan1 gim6 慳儉) parsimonious, close;
    • ꜀hán lun꜅ (haan1 leon6 慳吝) (or nun꜅ (neon6)) stingy;
    • ꜀hán lik꜇ (haan1 lik6 慳力) saving of strength;
    • ꜂hò ꜀hán (hou2 haan1 好慳), closefisted;
    • ꜀hán ꜀fán (haan1 faan1 慳返), saved the expense, spared the trouble.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【慳】 (haan1)
    1. To be frugal, to economise.
      • 慳水 (haan1 seoi2): "Save water."
      • 慳錢 (haan1 cin4-2): "Save money."
      • 慳皮 (haan1 pei4-2): "Be frugal."
      • 慳電膽 (haan1 din6 daam2): "Energy-saving [light]-bulb" (i.e. a compact fluorescent lamp).

In Cantonese, 慳 is often used positively (to mean "frugal" rather than "stingy"), e.g. in the compliment 知慳識儉.
