  • 慳

慳 ꜀Hán (haan1)

Radical + residual strokes
⼼ + 11
Unicode code point
U+6173
Alternative forms
  • 悭 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 67 慳) 香閒切吝嗇也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜀慳)
    • Saving, niggardly, stingy;
    • to be sparing of;
    • ꜀hán kím꜅ (haan1 gim6 慳儉) parsimonious, close;
    • ꜀hán lun꜅ (haan1 leon6 慳吝) (or nun꜅ (neon6)) stingy;
    • ꜀hán lik꜇ (haan1 lik6 慳力) saving of strength;
    • ꜂hò ꜀hán (hou2 haan1 好慳), closefisted;
    • ꜀hán ꜀fán (haan1 faan1 慳返), saved the expense, spared the trouble.
Conway's commentary
  • In Cantonese, 慳 is usually used positively to mean "frugal" rather than "stingy" (e.g. in the compliment 知慳識儉).
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【慳】 (haan1)
    1. To be frugal, to economise.
      • 慳水 (haan1 seoi2): "Save water."
      • 慳錢 (haan1 cin4-2): "Save money."
      • 慳皮 (haan1 pei4-2): "Be frugal."
      • 慳電膽 (haan1 din6 daam2): "Energy-saving [light]-bulb" (i.e. a compact fluorescent lamp).

閑 ꜁Hán (haan4)

Radical + residual strokes
⾨ + 4
Unicode code point
U+9591
Alternative forms
  • 闲 (Mainland simplified)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 68 閑) [闌]也止防也又[散]也[習]也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜁閑)
    • A bar or barrier, a fence (Kangxi: 闌也 etc., 閑與梐枑皆禁衞之物也);
    • an inclosure;
    • a fold or stable for horses (Kangxi: 馬闌也 etc.);
    • to close, to obstruct (Kangxi: 閑閡也, 閑遮也);
    • to protect or to guard against (Kangxi: 防也禦也法也);
    • to regulate by law, to forbid;
    • to move about (Kangxi: 閑閑動搖也);
    • large (Kangxi: 大也);
    • accustomed, practiced (Kangxi: 習也);
    • ꜁hán ꜂cho (haan4 zo2 閑阻), to embarrass;
    • ꜁hán tsáp꜇ (haan4 zaap6 閑習) broken in, used to.

    (…)

    (Alternative form of 嫺 (for the "adept" sense). See 嫺 haan4.)

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 閒. See 閒 haan4 for the canonical entry.)

Conway's commentary
  • Fan Wan entry: Fixed missing strokes in 闌 and 習, and normalised 㪚 to 散.
  • Williams' "embarrass": In the (now) archaic sense of "impede", "obstruct".
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【閑】 (hean)
    • (noun-like) guardrail
    • (noun-like) enclosure
    • (verb-like) [to] guard (against)
    • (adjective-like) great
    • (adjective-like) [well]-trained

閒 ꜁Hán (haan4)

Radical + residual strokes
⾨ + 4
Unicode code point
U+9592
Alternative forms
  • 閑
  • 間
  • 闲 (Mainland simplified)
Reading variations
  • haan4-2 (rising second in vernacular 閒閒地)
Fan Wan entry
  • (Vol. 2 Image 68 閒) 暇也安逸也
Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 72 ꜁閒)
    • Repose (Kangxi: 安也), leisure (Kangxi: 𨻶也, […]空閒[…]);
    • indolent;
    • unoccupied, empty;
    • tak꜆ ꜁hán (dak1 haan4 得閒), at leisure;
    • ꜁mò ꜁hán (mou4 haan4 無閒), busy;
    • ꜁hán ꜁yau (haan4 jau4 閒遊), sauntering about;
    • ꜁hán ꜂shau (haan4 sau2 閒手), an idler;
    • ꜁hán wá꜅ (haan4 waa6-2 閒話) small talk;
    • ꜁hán sz'꜅ (haan4 si6 閒事) private, trifling affairs;
    • ꜁yau ꜂shau hò꜄ ꜁hán (jau4 sau2 hou3 haan4 遊手好閒), a vagrant loves leisure;
    • ꜁hán uk꜆ (haan4 uk1 閒屋) an untenanted house;
    • ꜁hán tí꜅ (haan4 dei6 閒地)) a void place, room;
    • ꜀chʻau ꜁hán (cau1 haan4 抽閒), to take time for;
    • ꜁fong ꜁hán (fong4 haan4 防閒), to look out for idlers.

    (Otherwise, an alternative form of 嫺 (for the "adept" sense). See 嫺 haan4 for the canonical entry.)

  • (Page 72 ꜁閑)

    (…)

    Often used for the preceding (閒).

Conway's supplementary entry
  1. Irrelevant.
    • 閒人免進 (haan4 jan4 min5 zeon3): "Irrelevant persons [shall] avoid proceeding" (i.e. no unauthorised entry).
  2. Ordinary.
    • 閒日 (haan4 jat6-2): "Ordinary day" (i.e. weekday).
Conway's literary rendering
  • 【閒】 (hean)
    • (noun-like) idle-time
    • (adjective-like) idle, idling
    • (verb-like) (met.) [to] calm
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【閒閒地】 (haan4 haan4-2 dei6-2)
    1. Used to indicate the lower end of expenditure, time required, etc. (Literally, "[even] ordinarily".)
      • 閒閒地都要廿蚊 (haan4 haan4-2 dei6-2 dou1 jiu3 jaa6 man1): "Even at [the] lower end, [you'd] need [to pay] twenty bucks."
