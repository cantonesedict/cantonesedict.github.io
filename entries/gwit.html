Williams (1856) entry
  • (Page 214 #1: Kwít꜆ (gwit1) exemplified; here assigned to 𠵯)
    • A squeaking noise at night, called kwít꜆ ꜀sheng (gwit1 seng1 𠵯聲), is thought to be the cry of ghosts;
    • the squeaking of mice;
    • clever, astute, sagacious;
    • kòm꜄ kwít꜆ (gam3 gwit1 咁𠵯) so smart—said of children.
Conway's Cantonese entry
  • 【𠵯】 (gwit1)
    1. (onomatopoeia) A squeak.

The spelling 𠵯 is arbitrary.
